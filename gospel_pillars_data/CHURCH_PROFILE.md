## 6. Discipleship & Membership Integration: Next Pillar Association (NPA)
The church organizes spiritual growth into three progressive levels:
1.  **Classical (Paul's) Class:** Designed for first-time guests, new converts, and believers laying foundation in church vision, basic doctrine, and salvation.
2.  **Executive (Philip's) Class:** Tailored online/flexible modules for busy professionals, corporate executives, and distance learners.
3.  **Presidential (Cornelius) Class:** Advanced leadership, spiritual maturation, and ministerial commissioning track.

## 7. Global Outreach Arms
*   **Mission Assisted Project (MAP):** Raises, equips, and finances missionaries sent to unreached tribes and territories globally.
*   **Higher Impact Club (HIC):** Career, corporate networking, and business development arm for Christian entrepreneurs and professionals.
*   **Royalty Academy:** Business education and skill acquisition academy equipping "Money Missionaries."
*   **Kingdom Kids:** Dedicated children's church nurturing children and pre-teens in scripture and character.
*   **OneSound Choir:** Anointed praise and worship team producing original gospel worship recordings.
