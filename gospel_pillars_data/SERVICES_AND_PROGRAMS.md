## 2. Service Elements & What to Expect

### Sunday Glory Celebrations
*   **Atmosphere:** Majestic, orderly, spirit-filled, and joyful.
*   **Music Ministry:** Led by the OneSound Choir and orchestra, fusing contemporary praise, traditional hymns, and original prophetic sounds.
*   **Sermon Ministry:** Systematic scripture exposition delivered by Prophet Dr. Isaiah Macwealth or resident ministers.
*   **Ministry Time:** Altar calls, salvation prayers, laying on of hands, and testimonies of healing.

### Midweek Communion Service (Wednesdays)
*   Focuses on discipleship, doctrine, and understanding spiritual laws.
*   Administration of the Holy Communion to partake of Christ's supernatural life and victory over disease, stagnation, and oppression.

### Friday "Reblaze" Revival Meeting
*   An intensive atmosphere of fire, prayer, breaking of generational curses, and restoration of spiritual gifts.

## 3. Annual Flagship Conferences & Gatherings

1.  **Greater Glory Gathering (G3):**
    *   Annual flagship global convention hosting delegates, pastors, and worshippers from across Nigeria, the UK, the USA, Ghana, South Africa, and Europe.
    *   Features international music ministers (e.g. Sinach, Ebuka Songs) and guest apostolic fathers.
2.  **Higher Impact Conference:**
    *   Empowerment conference for leaders, business builders, career executives, and innovators.
3.  **Miracle & Prophetic Crusades:**
    *   Large-scale regional crusades bringing salvation and signs & wonders to unreached cities.
